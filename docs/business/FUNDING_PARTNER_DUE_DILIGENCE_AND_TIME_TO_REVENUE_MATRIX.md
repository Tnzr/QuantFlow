# Funding Partner Due Diligence and Time-to-Revenue Matrix

Date: 2026-06-18
Status: Execution template
Audience: Founders, finance, partnerships, and go-to-market operators

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a practical scoring model to compare lenders, funding partners, and strategic channels based on:
- Time to first capital
- Time to first paying client impact
- Net effect on gross margin and runway
- Compliance and operational risk

Use it to prioritize capital and partner pathways that reduce time to revenue while preserving governance quality.

## 2. Definitions

- Time to Capital (TTC): Days from outreach start to funds available.
- Time to Revenue (TTR): Days from agreement start to first attributable paid customer.
- Time to Client (TTCli): Days from agreement start to first qualified customer onboarded.
- Revenue Quality Score (RQS): Composite score for retention, expansion, and support burden.

## 3. Candidate Source Categories

- Angel and strategic seed investors
- Accelerators and grants
- Revenue-based financing providers
- Venture debt and working capital lenders
- Channel partners (educators, communities, affiliates)
- Ecosystem finance partners (context-only, not core financing)

## 4. Weighted Scoring Model

## 4.1 Weight Defaults

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Speed to capital (TTC) | 15 | Lower days is better |
| Speed to first client (TTCli) | 20 | Directly affects demand validation |
| Speed to first revenue (TTR) | 20 | Directly affects runway pressure |
| Capital cost and dilution | 15 | Total economic cost |
| Revenue quality impact (RQS) | 15 | Retention and support burden |
| Compliance and legal fit | 10 | Regulatory and disclosure readiness |
| Operational complexity | 5 | Integration and execution overhead |

Total: 100%

## 4.2 Scoring Scale

Use 1-5 for each criterion:
- 1 = poor
- 2 = weak
- 3 = acceptable
- 4 = strong
- 5 = excellent

Weighted Score formula:

$$
\text{Weighted Score} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (\text{Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i)
$$

Normalize to percentage by dividing by 5.

## 6. Lender and Partner Checklists

## 6.1 Commercial Checklist
- Effective APR, fees, and hidden covenants documented
- Payment schedule stress-tested against downside revenue case
- Termination clauses and penalty terms reviewed
- Data-sharing and exclusivity terms evaluated

## 6.2 Legal and Compliance Checklist
- Jurisdiction and licensing implications reviewed
- Marketing claims constraints and disclosure obligations captured
- Contract language reviewed for advisory-risk exposure
- Partner activities aligned with permitted business model

## 6.3 Operational Checklist
- Integration workload estimate (engineering days)
- Reporting and reconciliation requirements
- Support burden estimate
- Incident ownership and escalation path

## 6.4 Channel Quality Checklist
- Audience fit with target investor segments
- Historical conversion quality (not only volume)
- Retention profile of referred cohorts
- Brand trust and reputation risk assessment

## 9. Suggested Funding and Partner Sequence

## Phase A (0-90 Days)
- Prioritize low-friction channels with strongest TTCli and TTR profile.
- Focus on partner types with high trust and high-quality referrals.
- Keep debt exposure minimal until paid cohort reliability is validated.

## Phase B (3-9 Months)
- Add selective non-dilutive and structured financing once retention and revenue quality stabilize.
- Expand channel partners with cohort-level economics proof.

## Phase C (9-18 Months)
- Use larger capital instruments only after unit economics and governance controls are proven.

## 10. Red Flags

Reject or pause candidates when any applies:
- Unclear fee stack or restrictive covenants
- Contractual claims that create regulatory exposure
- Partner traffic quality that does not retain
- Integration burden that delays TTR beyond target threshold

## 11. Governance and Review Cadence

Weekly:
- Update funnel, TTCli, and TTR metrics
- Review blockers and legal/commercial status

Monthly:
- Re-score all active candidates
- Reallocate effort to top quartile partners by weighted score

Quarterly:
- Update weighting model based on realized economics and compliance learning

## 12. Quick-Start Instructions

1. List 10 candidate funding/partner pathways.
2. Fill TTC, TTCli, and TTR estimates for each.
3. Score each criterion (1-5) and calculate weighted score.
4. Advance top 3 candidates with best speed and quality profile.
5. Run first cohort and measure actual TTR delta vs estimate.
6. Feed results back into weights and next-quarter prioritization.

This template is designed to make time-to-client and time-to-revenue decision-making explicit, comparable, and execution-ready.
