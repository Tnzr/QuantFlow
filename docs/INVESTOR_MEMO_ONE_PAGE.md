# QuantFlow Investor Memo (One Page)

Date: 2026-06-18

## Opportunity
QuantFlow is building a subscription intelligence service for swing and breakout expectations. The platform fuses technical indicators, seasonality, and sentiment into explainable signal narratives designed for self-directed and semi-professional investors.

## Why Now
Investor tooling is increasingly AI-assisted, but most workflows remain fragmented and opaque. Users need integrated signal context and auditable risk framing, not just isolated alerts.

## Product
QuantFlow provides:
- Signal generation with rationale and confidence context
- Backtesting continuity with practical interpretation layers
- Planned service controls for reliability, governance, and transparency

## Revenue Model
Tiered subscription:
- Starter: delayed insights
- Pro: near-real-time contextual signals
- Premium: advanced strategy and portfolio capabilities

## Current Status
Completed:
- Signal and recommendation baseline
- Contextual backtest integration
- UX clarity improvements for key workflows
- Technical assessment and implementation blueprint

Next:
- Stripe-first subscription integration
- Entitlement enforcement across API and UI
- Audit and strategy-version lineage
- Controlled paid beta

## Competitive Edge
- Context-rich signal narratives rather than black-box outputs
- Explicit invalidation and risk framing
- Planned auditability and compliance-forward architecture

## Risk and Mitigation
Top risks:
- Regulatory misalignment
- Live performance drift
- Monetization-control failures

Mitigations:
- Legal review gates
- Runtime guardrails and kill-switches
- Idempotent billing event architecture

## Solidarity Fund (Roadmap Intent)
QuantFlow plans a future, opt-in solidarity contribution mechanism where users may route a configurable percentage of their net growth profits into vetted philanthropic pipelines (for example NGOs, local education programs, and approved impact vehicles).

This is a documented roadmap concept and is not yet implemented.

## Ask
Support QuantFlow in converting MVP capability into a production-grade, investor-trustworthy subscription service through commercialization hardening, compliance readiness, and disciplined go-to-market execution.
