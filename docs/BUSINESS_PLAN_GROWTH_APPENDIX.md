# QuantFlow Business Plan Growth Appendix (Living Document)

Date: 2026-06-18
Purpose: Expandable appendix to evolve from current investor memo into a full business plan

## 1. Business Plan Scope
This appendix defines the structure and first-pass content for a full business plan that can be iterated each quarter.

## 2. Core Planning Modules

### 2.1 Market and Customer
- TAM/SAM/SOM by investor segment
- Persona segmentation and willingness-to-pay analysis
- Competitor benchmarking and positioning map

### 2.2 Product and Roadmap
- Feature milestones by quarter
- Reliability and trust milestones
- Compliance posture milestones by jurisdiction

### 2.3 Monetization and Pricing
- Tier pricing hypotheses
- Trial design and conversion strategy
- Churn and retention playbooks

### 2.4 Distribution and GTM
- Direct acquisition channels
- Partner channels and affiliates
- Community-led growth pathways

### 2.5 Financial Model
- Revenue projections (base/upside/downside)
- Cost structure (infrastructure, data, compliance, support)
- Cash runway and hiring plan

### 2.6 Risk, Governance, and Controls
- Regulatory and legal risk inventory
- Model and data quality risks
- Operational and cybersecurity controls

## 3. 12-Month Milestone Framework

Q1:
- Billing + entitlement MVP
- Paid beta launch
- Baseline KPI instrumentation

Q2:
- Retention optimization
- Strategy governance automation
- Compliance collateral maturity

Q3:
- API and partner distribution pilots
- Geographic expansion readiness checks
- Enhanced reporting and enterprise hooks

Q4:
- Scale economics optimization
- New product line evaluation
- Institutional-grade trust package

## 4. KPI Spine for Plan Governance

Commercial:
- Trial-to-paid conversion
- Net revenue retention
- Monthly churn

Product:
- Time-to-first-value
- Active usage frequency
- Feature adoption by tier

Reliability:
- Uptime
- p95 latency
- Billing-event processing success

Quant quality:
- Signal hit rate by regime
- Drawdown control
- Performance stability over rolling windows

Trust:
- Incident count and response time
- User clarity and confidence feedback
- Disclosure and policy acceptance

## 5. Solidarity Fund Expansion Track (Future)

## 5.1 Concept
A user-opt-in mechanism enabling users to direct a configurable percent of net growth profits to philanthropic pipelines.

## 5.2 Eligible Pipeline Types
- NGOs and non-profits
- Local education programs
- Approved impact or private vehicles with published due diligence standards

## 5.3 Governance Requirements Before Launch
- Legal and tax review by jurisdiction
- Transparency policy and quarterly reporting template
- Provider onboarding and vetting framework
- User controls for opt-in, percentage caps, and cancellation

## 5.4 Product Requirements (Future)
- Profit-calculation policy and disclosure
- Contribution scheduling and reconciliation ledger
- Recipient selection and allocation controls
- Audit trail and annual impact statement export

## 6. Decision Log Template
- Decision ID
- Date
- Owner
- Decision
- Alternatives considered
- Expected impact
- Review date

## 7. Quarterly Update Process
1. Refresh KPI baselines.
2. Update plan assumptions and risks.
3. Adjust roadmap and resource allocation.
4. Publish executive summary delta.

This appendix should be treated as a living business planning artifact and updated continuously as market feedback and operational data accumulate.
